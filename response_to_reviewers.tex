constructive comments. In the revised manuscript, we substantially narrowed the
claims and repositioned Camerala as a browser-native, local-first implementation
example for task-aligned physiological feature logging under limited,
task-constrained home/laboratory conditions. We address Reviewer 1's required
Conditions 1--13 below. Reviewer 2 recommended acceptance without revision.
